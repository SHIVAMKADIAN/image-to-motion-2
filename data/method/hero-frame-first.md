1. Generate 5-10 cheap image variations; pick the closest.
2. Refine that one image (inpaint / edit) until it is exactly right.
3. Animate ONCE from the approved hero frame.

Spend more on cheap image generations, far less on expensive video re-rolls. Change one variable at a time between generations.
