Traditional storyboard → my process
Draw the shot → visualise the shot in words
Lock the drawing → generate the visual
Adjust the drawing → adjust the prompt / reference
Shoot from the drawing → use the approved visual as the target for the video generation

A generated frame is easier for non-filmmakers to understand than a sketch.
