Every visible background person is a live performer, not a still image. Start each shot with activity already in progress. Soft focus changes detail, never motion. In a 6-10 second populated shot, let one or two extras complete different small actions at separate moments (a head turn, shoulder settling, a hand sliding from knee to chair arm) while the others breathe naturally. For shorter cuts, carry an action across the cut. Keep activity quieter than the speaker. No synchronized nodding, repeated loops, newly invented speech, frozen bokeh crowds, or motion limited to flames and smoke. Do not add people to empty or private scenes.
