category: method
medium: [image, video]
models: [any]

source: creator-handover-guide
---
- Natural skin texture.
- Realistic fabric, glass, metal, walls, roads and other materials.
- Lighting that belongs to the location and time of day.
- Natural depth between foreground, subject and background.
- Small imperfections instead of perfect surfaces everywhere.
- Simple, believable body movement.
- Objects having weight and interacting correctly with the environment.
- Avoiding unnecessary visual effects.

The test: "Could a real camera have captured this?"
