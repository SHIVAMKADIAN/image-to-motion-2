- Only the assigned speaker says the quoted line. Listeners react with eyes, breath and posture and do not mouth another person's line.
- Speak each line once. The pronunciation guide, English meaning and acting notes are silent — never spoken or repeated as a second line.
- Hyphens separate syllables for guidance; deliver connected natural speech, not syllable-by-syllable. Capitals mark articulation and long vowels, not shouting.
- Keep aspirated consonants (bh, dh, th, chh, gh); "th" is not the English sound in "think". Preserve nasal vowels (main, mein, hain, hoon).
- Dialogue windows include pauses; finish words before the next speaker begins.
- Ambient motion continues during emotional pauses: breathing, cloth, flames, smoke. No slow-motion lip sync.
- Emotion is a thought changing in response to the previous event: notice, react, then speak.
