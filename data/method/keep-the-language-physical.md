title: Keep the Language Physical
category: method
medium: [image, video]

---
Models respond to what a person, camera or object actually does.
"He looks powerful" is weak. Describe posture, expression, movement and framing instead: "He sits upright, shoulders square, chin level, eyes steady on the door; the camera is low and slowly pushes in."

Replace: "make it emotional" → what the person's face and body do. "Cinematic" → the actual camera.
