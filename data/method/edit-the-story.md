- Choose the take that communicates the story best.
- Wide shots establish place and relationships.
- Close-ups when the audience needs to notice a face or reaction.
- Detail shots when an object or clue matters.
- Movement shots connect locations or create transitions.
- Dialogue, ambience and music connect the visual pieces.
- Remove shots that look good but slow down or confuse the story.

Viewer test: do I understand what is happening? Do I know where I am? Do I know who matters? Do I know what to notice?
