category: method
medium: [image, video]

- Identify recurring locations.
- Identify important vehicles and objects.
- Keep the visual language of recurring places consistent.
- If the story has a specific time of day, keep the lighting believable across nearby shots.
- Only change a location when the story says the location has changed.

A consistent character in a house, road or vehicle that changes every shot still feels wrong.
