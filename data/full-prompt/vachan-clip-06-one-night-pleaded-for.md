00–08s — Shot 1: She understands
75mm Devaki notices his glance and gathers infant closer with supported forearms. 01–08s Devaki, pleading through controlled sobs: "Ek raatri. Bas ek raatri mere paas rehne dijiye."
Pronunciation guide: ek RAAT-ri. bas ek RAAT-ri ME-re paas REH-ne DEE-ji-ye.
English meaning (not spoken): Please let my child stay with me for just one night.
Acting and response (not spoken): Devaki senses he may take the baby away. Inner brows lift and draw together; she leans slightly toward Vasudev while keeping the infant stable. First Ek raatri is tentative; repeated Bas ek raatri grows urgent, the final request thins with a caught breath. Vasudev struggles to meet her eyes.

She addresses Vasudev, not camera.

08–15s — Shot 2: Promise as burden
65mm Vasudev, jaw tight, eyes unable to hold hers at first. 09–15s Vasudev: "Woh vachan ki prateeksha kar raha hoga, Devaki."
Pronunciation guide: wo VA-chan kee pra-TEEK-shaa kar ra-HAA HO-gaa, DE-va-kee.
English meaning (not spoken): Kans will be waiting for me to fulfil my promise.
Acting and response (not spoken): Vasudev explains a duty he hates. Begin looking down, force out vachan with a tight jaw, then meet her gaze on her name. Voice low and burdened, not an official announcement. Hands remain empty. Devaki’s hopeful expression tightens as she understands his answer.

His hands stay empty in his lap; no premature transfer.

Her brow remains knitted; supporting arms stay fixed under baby. Vasudev inhales but cannot offer agreement.

Hold her searching eyes after sentence.
