Create a cinematic character reference sheet for ROHINI, for an Indian mythological film about Krishna’s birth.

Layout: four clearly separated photographic views on one landscape image—full-body front, full-body rear, waist-up three-quarter view, and a detailed face close-up. Exactly the same person, facial features, age, body proportions, clothing, jewelry and hairstyle in every panel. Plain neutral earth-colored background, clear readable illumination. The canvas contains photographic views only.

Rohini is an Indian woman approximately 30–36 years old, with medium-brown skin, a long oval face, thoughtful almond-shaped dark-brown eyes, a gently defined nose and softly arched brows. Her build is medium and her posture composed. Her expression carries maternal tenderness and a quiet private concern. Show her postpartum adult appearance.

Dress her in an earthy muted teal cotton-silk sari with a narrow antique-gold border and matching modest blouse. The pallu rests securely over her LEFT shoulder. Black hair is center-parted and arranged in a low braided bun. Small red bindi, modest gold earrings, one short necklace and a restrained set of bangles. Traditional flat sandals. Keep both hands empty and relaxed so this sheet defines Rohini herself. The sari provides practical seated movement and space for later cradling an infant.

Photorealistic Indian epic cinema. Earthy visual treatment with natural skin tones, tactile cloth, restrained warm gold, rich natural colors and substantial but readable shadows. Soft directional studio illumination reveals facial identity and fabric construction. Full-body views include the complete head and feet. Original individual face and believable human proportions.
