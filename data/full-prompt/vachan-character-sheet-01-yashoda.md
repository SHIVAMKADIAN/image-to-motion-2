Create a cinematic character reference sheet for YASHODA, for an Indian mythological film about Krishna’s birth.

Layout: four clearly separated photographic views on one landscape image—full-body front, full-body rear, waist-up three-quarter view, and a detailed face close-up. Exactly the same person, facial features, age, body proportions, clothing, jewelry and hairstyle in every panel. Plain neutral earth-colored background, clear readable illumination. The canvas contains photographic views only.

Yashoda is an Indian woman approximately 30–35 years old, with warm medium-brown skin, a softly rounded face, expressive dark-brown eyes, full cheeks and a welcoming maternal smile. Her build is sturdy and naturally full, with relaxed shoulders and practical movement.

Dress her in a warm turmeric-ochre cotton sari with a restrained deep-red woven border and a matching deep-red short-sleeved blouse. The sari pallu sits securely over her LEFT shoulder, leaving both hands free for churning butter. Center-parted black hair forms a compact low bun. Small red bindi, small gold earrings, a short simple gold necklace, coordinated red-and-gold bangles and traditional flat sandals. Her household clothing has fine woven texture and comfortable folds. Keep her arms relaxed and hands empty.

Photorealistic Indian epic cinema. Earthy visual treatment with natural skin tones, tactile cloth, restrained warm gold, rich natural colors and substantial but readable shadows. Soft directional studio illumination reveals facial identity and fabric construction. Full-body views include the complete head and feet. Original individual face and believable human proportions.
