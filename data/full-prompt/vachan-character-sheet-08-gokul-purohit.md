Create a cinematic character reference sheet for GOKUL PUROHIT, for an Indian mythological film about Krishna’s birth.

Layout: four clearly separated photographic views on one landscape image—full-body front, full-body rear, waist-up three-quarter view, and a detailed face close-up. Exactly the same person, facial features, age, body proportions, clothing, jewelry and hairstyle in every panel. Plain neutral earth-colored background, clear readable illumination. The canvas contains photographic views only.

An Indian man approximately 58–64 years old, with medium-brown skin, a broad square face, gentle deep-set brown eyes, a rounded nose and a short neatly maintained silver beard and moustache. Shaved scalp with a small gray shikha at the rear. Three narrow pale horizontal forehead lines with a small central red dot. Calm, attentive and kindly expression.

Warm ivory cotton dhoti with a narrow muted ochre border, a pale yellow shoulder cloth over LEFT shoulder, visible sacred thread and one small wooden-bead necklace. Bare feet. Both hands empty and relaxed. Preserve his square face and short silver beard as distinct from the younger clean-shaven wedding priest, the long-bearded Ugrasena and the turbaned Devak.

Photorealistic Indian epic cinema. Earthy visual treatment with natural skin tones, tactile cloth, restrained warm gold, rich natural colors and substantial but readable shadows. Soft directional studio illumination reveals facial identity and fabric construction. Full-body views include the complete head and feet. Original individual face and believable human proportions.
