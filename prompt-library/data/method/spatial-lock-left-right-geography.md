---
title: Spatial Lock Left-Right Geography
category: method
medium: [video]
models: [seedance-2]
tags: [geography, blocking, left-right, continuity, axis, spatial]
notes: The single most useful habit for multi-shot generations with several characters.
source: vachan production prompts (generalized)
---
1. Declare every location's layout once, from a stated camera position: what is on the LEFT, what is on the RIGHT, where entrances and light sources are.
2. Use capitalised LEFT / RIGHT and say whose left (anatomical vs frame) when it matters.
3. "A change of camera angle never moves an actor." A closer view crops the existing arrangement.
4. Keep the camera on one side of the axis within a scene; for dialogue place the listener on the speaker's established eyeline and keep a listener shoulder or landmark in frame.
5. Movement finishes in the stated destination before the next action begins.
6. Apply each location's geography only in the shots where that location appears.
7. Restate seats, standing places, facing directions, occupied hands and object ownership before each cut.
