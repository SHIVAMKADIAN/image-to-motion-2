title: Common Beginner Mistakes
category: method
medium: [image, video]
models: [any]
tags: [mistakes, beginner, pitfalls]
source: creator-handover-guide
---
- Starting with a prompt before understanding the scene.
- Trying to make every shot spectacular instead of readable.
- Changing a character's face because one generation looks nicer.
- Putting several unrelated actions into one shot.
- Vague instructions ("make it emotional") without describing what the person does.
- Saying "cinematic" without explaining the actual camera.
- Adding random background characters or objects.
- Changing a recurring location from shot to shot.
- Trying to fix five problems at the same time.
- Keeping a beautiful shot that tells the story incorrectly.
