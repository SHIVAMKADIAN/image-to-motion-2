Specify ONE primary camera move per shot. Stacking moves (dolly + pan + tilt) is the leading cause of jitter, unwanted rotation and failed generations.

Wrong: "dolly push forward while panning left and tilting up to reveal the skyline"
Right: "slow dolly push from medium shot to tight close-up over 8 seconds"

Need several moves? Split into separate shots, or state a timed sequence ("pans left 0-3s, holds, pushes in 4-8s").
