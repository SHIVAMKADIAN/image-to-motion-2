---
title: Infant Handling Continuity
category: method
medium: [video]
models: [seedance-2]
tags: [infant, baby, safety, handover, continuity, props]
notes: Reduces the most common failure modes (limb distortion, teleporting baby, swaddle changes).
source: vachan production prompts (generalized)
---
- Keep head/neck and hip support continuous in every shot; preserve head-to-feet orientation across cuts.
- One handover only, directly from carrier A to carrier B; a third person may reach but never takes ownership.
- Convey force through authority and the parent's distress, never by tugging the infant's limbs or neck.
- Keep a separate keepsake or cloth visibly distinct from the swaddle and track who holds it and where it lands.
- Close-ups on the infant crop the same ongoing action; swaddle unchanged.
