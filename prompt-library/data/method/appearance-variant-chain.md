1. Lock the base identity sheet first.
2. Generate variants in story order, attaching the approved base (or the previous variant) each time.
3. State what to preserve, what to change, and what must stay unchanged — in three explicit lists.
4. Treat wear, aging (years) and retained jewelry as provisional until accepted renders are checked against the final script.
5. Put restraints that are wearable (cuffs, slack chain) on the sheet; put lock states and tethers in the video prompt.
6. Keep each variant's face recognisable as the same person; change posture, expression, wear — not bone structure.
