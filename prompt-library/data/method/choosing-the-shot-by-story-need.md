Ask: "If I could not use words here, what would I show the audience?"

Where we are → wide or aerial establishing shot
Who is with whom → two-shot / medium shot
A character's reaction → close-up
A specific object or clue → detail / insert shot
Someone travelling → vehicle / movement shot
A reveal → controlled camera move, or a shot that hides then reveals
A change in perspective → POV / inside-vehicle / over-the-shoulder

These are ways of thinking about what information the viewer needs, not rules.
