For each scene, ask:
- Who is there?
- Where are they?
- What is happening?
- What does the audience need to understand?
- What is the most important action?
- What should the audience notice?
- What is the emotional tone?
- What needs to match the next or previous shot?

Then split into visual moments: one visual moment = one thing the audience needs to see.
