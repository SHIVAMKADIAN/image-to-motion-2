Create a cinematic character reference sheet for MAHARAJ UGRASENA DURING IMPRISONMENT, for an Indian mythological film about Krishna’s birth.

Use the attached approved reference to preserve the character’s exact facial identity and established design while applying only the appearance changes described below.

Layout: four clearly separated photographic views on one landscape image—full-body front, full-body rear, waist-up three-quarter view, and a detailed face close-up. Exactly the same person, facial features, age, body proportions, clothing, jewelry and hairstyle in every panel. Plain neutral earth-colored background, clear readable illumination. The canvas contains photographic views only.

Preserve Ugrasena’s recognizable elderly face, long white beard and moustache, forehead mark and gray-white hair from the attached reference. His build is lean and his shoulders slightly stooped. The expression is sorrowful, reflective and attentive. His head is uncovered, with natural gray-white hair gathered loosely behind.

Dress him in coarse faded ivory cotton dhoti and a plain muted gray-beige shoulder cloth. Bare feet. His wrists wear heavy aged-iron cuffs joined by a slack chain. Keep hands relaxed and visible. The costume communicates an imprisoned former ruler through simplicity, wear and dignified posture. Maintain his established long beard rather than using the shorter beard of the Gokul priest.

Photorealistic Indian epic cinema. Earthy visual treatment with natural skin tones, tactile cloth, restrained warm gold, rich natural colors and substantial but readable shadows. Soft directional studio illumination reveals facial identity and fabric construction. Full-body views include the complete head and feet. Original individual face and believable human proportions.
