Create a cinematic character reference sheet for KIRTIMAN, DEVAKI’S FIRST NEWBORN SON, for an Indian mythological film about Krishna’s birth.

Layout: four clearly separated photographic views on one landscape image—supported full-length front view, supported full-length side view, supported three-quarter upper-body view, and detailed face close-up. Exactly the same infant, age, facial features, hair and wrapping cloth in every panel. Every pose is safely reclining with full head and neck support on a plain padded cream surface. Plain neutral earth-colored background and clear gentle illumination. The canvas contains photographic views only.

Kirtiman is a newborn Indian boy with warm medium-brown skin, a small rounded face, fine short black hair, softly closed dark eyes, a small rounded nose and naturally curled tiny fingers. His cheeks have a gentle newborn fullness. Expressions remain restful and natural.

Use one soft faded brick-red cotton wrap with a narrow cream stitched edge, identifying the small cloth prepared by Devaki. Maintain the wrap weave, border and folds consistently. Show him safely supported on a plain padded cream surface in every view, with the face uncovered and the head and neck supported. The body is comfortably wrapped; one tiny hand may rest beside his cheek.

Photorealistic Indian epic cinema. Natural infant skin texture, soft directional illumination, tactile cotton, gentle readable shadows and believable newborn proportions. Preserve the infant’s identity and cloth design across all views.
