TIMED SHOTS:

00–09s — Shot 1: Terror becomes cruelty
65mm close-up of Kans standing beside his throne, alone within the frame. For the first half-second torchlight trembles across his eyes as he looks toward the abandoned demonstration. His jaw locks; he snaps his attention toward his men and roars the call, then delivers the threat in forceful intelligible clauses.
KANS: "Prahari! Pratham baalak wapas lao. Devaki ki aath santaan mein se ek bhi jeevit nahi rahegi!"
Pronunciation guide: pra-HA-ree! PRA-tham BAA-lak WAA-pas LAA-o. DE-va-kee kee aath san-TAAN mein se ek bhee JEE-vit na-HEE ra-HE-gee!
English meaning (silent): Bring the first child back; none of Devaki's eight children will remain alive.
Acting (silent): Sudden explosive Prahari, then clipped authority. Fear sharpens into deliberate cruelty on ek bhi. Chest rises, nostrils flare, one hand grips the throne back. Finish with cold certainty rather than a sustained scream.

09–12s — Shot 2: The old courtier objects
65mm old courtier seated in his SAME WEST front-row chair. He leans forward, eyebrows raised in alarm, one hand lifting a few inches from his knee.
OLD COURTIER: "Par Maharaj, aapne swayam use…"
Pronunciation guide: par ma-ha-RAAJ, AAP-ne swa-YAM U-se…
English meaning (silent): But you yourself had just spared him.
Acting (silent): Breath-worn elderly voice, tentative respectful objection. His sentence falters as Kans turns toward him. Remain seated.

12–19s — Shot 3: Pride answers
65mm Kans turns his gaze toward the courtier's actual chair. A brief hesitation exposes fear before he hardens.
KANS: "Agar un par daya ki… toh Mathura ko lagega Kans durbal ho gaya. Nahi!"
Pronunciation guide: a-GAR un par da-YAA kee… toh MA-thu-raa ko la-GE-gaa kans DUR-bal ho ga-YAA. na-HEE!
English meaning (silent): Mercy would make Mathura think Kans has become weak.
Acting (silent): Begin low and defensive, tighten the jaw on durbal, then strike Nahi with a short angry burst. Courtier lowers his raised hand; Pralamb becomes alert in his unchanged position.

19–21s — Shot 4: Obedience
50mm Pralamb at his established step-side mark, eyes on Kans.
PRALAMB: "Ji, Maharaj."
Pronunciation guide: jee, ma-ha-RAAJ.
English meaning (silent): Yes, my king.
Acting (silent): One decisive head inclination, disciplined low voice. After speaking he turns toward the entrance; Guard A follows. Cut as their departure begins.

21–26s — Shot 5: The order travels
Explicit cut to later at the prison corridor. Low 50mm tracking beside three pairs of boots: Pralamb leading, Guard A behind and Guard B last. Fast purposeful walking, soles striking damp stone. Torch sconces pass in parallax, real flames fluttering in the draft. Camera rises slightly at the end to establish their approach to the closed cell gate. Guard B's spear stays upright. Footfalls continue across the next cut.

26–30s — Shot 6: The parents hear them
50mm inside-cell side medium: Devaki seated LEFT holding Kirtiman; Vasudev seated at her RIGHT. He hears approaching boots, turns toward the RIGHT doorway, rises and takes one short step into the clear lane between her and the gate. His empty cuffed hands stay low. Devaki's breath catches; she gathers the supported infant close. Keep stitched cloth on her knees. End with gate still closed and Guard A visible outside reaching toward its lock.
