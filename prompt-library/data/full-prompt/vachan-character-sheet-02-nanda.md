Create a cinematic character reference sheet for NANDA, for an Indian mythological film about Krishna’s birth.

Layout: four clearly separated photographic views on one landscape image—full-body front, full-body rear, waist-up three-quarter view, and a detailed face close-up. Exactly the same person, facial features, age, body proportions, clothing, jewelry and hairstyle in every panel. Plain neutral earth-colored background, clear readable illumination. The canvas contains photographic views only.

Nanda is an Indian man approximately 40–45 years old, tall with a broad sturdy build, warm medium-brown skin, a broad friendly face, deep-set kind dark-brown eyes, thick black moustache and neatly trimmed short beard. His expression is cheerful, reassuring and grounded.

Dress him in a cream cotton dhoti with a muted mustard border, a rust-brown sleeveless waistcoat and a warm ochre turban tied in one fixed practical arrangement. A cream shoulder cloth rests over his LEFT shoulder. Simple gold earrings, one modest neck ornament, a woven waist sash and brown leather sandals. His RIGHT hand holds one plain weathered wooden cattle-herding staff vertically beside him in full-body views. Preserve staff shape, turban folds and clothing across panels.

Photorealistic Indian epic cinema. Earthy visual treatment with natural skin tones, tactile cloth, restrained warm gold, rich natural colors and substantial but readable shadows. Soft directional studio illumination reveals facial identity and fabric construction. Full-body views include the complete head and feet. Original individual face and believable human proportions.
