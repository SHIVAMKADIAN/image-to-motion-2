Create a cinematic character reference sheet for DEVARSHI NARADA WITH VEENA, for an Indian mythological film about Krishna’s birth.

Layout: four clearly separated photographic views on one landscape image—full-body front, full-body rear, waist-up three-quarter view, and a detailed face close-up. Exactly the same person, facial features, age, body proportions, clothing, jewelry and hairstyle in every panel. Plain neutral earth-colored background, clear readable illumination. The canvas contains photographic views only.

An Indian man appearing approximately 55, lean build, medium-brown skin, a long oval face, high forehead, gentle attentive dark-brown eyes and a straight nose. Clean-shaven cheeks and chin. Silver-gray hair gathered into a neat high topknot. Restrained white U-shaped forehead tilak with a fine red central line. Calm, observant and assured expression. Give him an independent face distinct from the approved old courtier.

Muted saffron drape over LEFT shoulder and warm ivory dhoti with a narrow ochre border. One simple tulsi-bead necklace and plain wooden sandals. One practical wooden veena with a long dark neck and rounded polished resonator is supported diagonally at his LEFT shoulder, keeping the face visible. Use a consistent simple instrument design. His RIGHT forearm is free. The reference establishes instrument and carrying arrangement; putting it aside before the tray demonstration belongs in the video blocking.

Photorealistic Indian epic cinema. Earthy visual treatment with natural skin tones, tactile cloth, restrained warm gold, rich natural colors and substantial but readable shadows. Soft directional studio illumination reveals facial identity and fabric construction. Full-body views include the complete head and feet. Original individual face and believable human proportions.
