Create a cinematic character reference sheet for BALRAM, ROHINI’S NEWBORN SON, for an Indian mythological film about Krishna’s birth.

Layout: four clearly separated photographic views on one landscape image—supported full-length front view, supported full-length side view, supported three-quarter upper-body view, and detailed face close-up. Exactly the same infant, age, facial features, hair and wrapping cloth in every panel. Every pose is safely reclining with full head and neck support on a plain padded cream surface. Plain neutral earth-colored background and clear gentle illumination. The canvas contains photographic views only.

Balram is a newborn Indian boy with light warm-brown skin, a broad softly rounded face, full cheeks, fine dark hair and a small gently rounded chin. He has a slightly fuller newborn build than Kirtiman, with believable infant proportions. His expression is peaceful with a small natural change between resting and softly open eyes.

Use one soft warm-ivory cotton wrap with a narrow muted-indigo woven border. Preserve the wrap design and weave in every view. Show him safely supported on a plain padded cream surface, face uncovered and head and neck supported. One tiny hand can emerge at the upper edge of the wrap. His distinctive face and ivory-indigo cloth establish a separate identity from Kirtiman.

Photorealistic Indian epic cinema. Natural infant skin texture, soft directional illumination, tactile cotton, gentle readable shadows and believable newborn proportions. Preserve the infant’s identity and cloth design across all views.
